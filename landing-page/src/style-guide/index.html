Mark (icon): --color-text for structural shapes, --color-accent (copper) for the flash & accent dots, --color-accent-cool (steel blue) for the dynamic element

Border detail: --color-border for the subtle outline layer on the check mark

Line Heights

--leading-tight
1.1 Tight leading for large display headings that need compact spacing.
--leading-snug
1.25 Snug leading for headings and card titles with moderate spacing.
--leading-normal
1.5 Normal leading for body text. This is the default line height used throughout the site for comfortable reading at standard sizes.
--leading-relaxed
1.75 Relaxed leading for long-form content. Used in blog posts and descriptions where extra breathing room improves readability.
