# bsf5y Messaging Strategy

## One-Liner

Build Products, not prototypes.

## Core Position

We help you build a product - software that scales, pivots, and survives success. Not a prototype that needs to be rebuilt when things go right.

## The Point

The difference between a product and a prototype isn't polish or features - it's where the value lives.

Prototypes trap business logic inside the user interface. When you need to add an API, support a new platform, or hand the codebase to a new team, you discover the mess. The rules are scattered. The architecture fights you. You rebuild.

Products capture your core business logic in portable software that any interface can use. The core doesn't change when you add a mobile app or an API. It doesn't break when you scale. It doesn't confuse new developers.

We capture your core in portable software that survives scaling, pivots, funding rounds, and team changes.

## Key Messages

**Build it once.**
Products don't need to be rebuilt when you succeed. The architecture handles growth, pivots, and new delivery channels without starting over.

**Your idea is 10% of the code. We protect that 10%.**
Your unique differentiator - the thing that makes your business special - is a small fraction of the total system. Everything else is commodity infrastructure. We focus obsessively on capturing and protecting that 10%.

**Software that survives success.**
What happens when things go right? More users, investors want in, you need to hire, you need to pivot. Prototypes break under success. Products don't.

**Ask us where the business rules live.**
We can answer this question clearly. If a dev partner can't, that tells you what they're building.

## Voice

**Blunt, not aggressive.**
"That's a prototype. It won't scale." Not "Your developers are idiots."

**Confident, not salesy.**
We don't need to convince anyone. We state how things work.

**Teacher, not preacher.**
We explain the mechanics. We don't moralize about "doing things right."

**Sparse.**
Short sentences. No jargon. No weasel words. Say it and stop.

## What We Don't Sound Like

- Agency-speak: "We deliver innovative solutions that drive business value."
- Humble-brag: "Our world-class team of experts..."
- Hedge-everything: "We believe that in many cases, potentially..."
- Startup hype: "Revolutionary. Disruptive. Game-changing."

## The Uncomfortable Truth

You can pay to build it right, or pay to build it twice. We do the first one.

Cheaper and faster options exist because they skip the hard part. The hard part is identifying your core value and building it to last. That takes experienced engineers and costs more upfront. But you pay now or you pay later - and later costs more.

## Target Audience

Non-technical founders and executives shopping for a development partner. They haven't been burned yet. They're comparing options. They need to understand why the approach matters before they've experienced the alternative.

## Success Outcomes

Clients who work with us:
- Scale without rewrites
- Pivot without starting over
- Pass technical due diligence with investors
- Hire in-house teams who can understand and extend the codebase
