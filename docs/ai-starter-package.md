# AI Starter Package

10 hour plan to help bootstrap day-to-day AI usage. $1500

- Workflow Audit (2–3 hours). Reviewing your current daily operations to find repetitive bottlenecks (e.g., drafting proposals, summarizing calls, sorting leads, or basic data entry).

- Tool Selection & Sandbox Setup (3–4 hours). Matching your budget and data privacy needs to
  specific software (such as Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, etc) and building a few
  starter projects for your individual AI assisted workflows.

- Hands-On Team Training (3–4 hours). Live working sessions teaching your staff how to prompt
  effectively, verify outputs, and apply the new workflows to their actual daily tasks.
