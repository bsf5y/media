# Coding-Agent Trial

6-week guided trial to get an engineering team productive with coding agents. $1500 per cohort of up to 8 engineers.

One 2-hour session per week, 12 hours total.

- Week 1: Kit-Up & Kickoff (2 hours). All training. Get every participant's tools installed,
  configured, and working, with enough instruction to be productive from the first day.

- Weeks 2–6: Instruct & Share (2 hours each week).
  - Instruction (1 hour). Training, new techniques, and Q&A, building on what the group ran into
    the week before.
  - Show & Tell (1 hour). Each participant shares what went well and what didn't over the previous
    week, on their real work. The group learns from every participant's experience, not just the
    instructor's.

Outcome: a team that has used coding agents on its own codebase for six weeks, a shared sense of
what works and what doesn't, and a clear picture of what to do next — wider rollout, pipeline
hardening, or a move toward a fully AI-driven development process.
