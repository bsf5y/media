*From AI-assisted to AI-driven*

Giving engineers a coding assistant is step one. We help your team rebuild its development process

## For the Rest of Your Business

### AI Readiness & Data Classification
*Know what you have before an agent does*

We help you identify your information sources — what exists and where it lives — then guide your
team in developing a data classification policy and putting it into practice. Your people do the
classifying; they know what's sensitive and who should see it. We bring the framework and the
process. You get a policy your organization owns, a clear picture of what's safe for AI to use, and
the habits to keep it that way. This is usually the work that turns "not yet" into "yes."

### Knowledge Systems for Agents
*Data lakes, knowledge graphs, vector search (RAG)*

Agents are only as good as what they can find. We curate and structure your organization's
knowledge and expose it through the right retrieval method for the job — not the trendiest one.
You get agents that answer with your information, not guesses.

### Agentic Tools Training
*Recurring, hands-on, for everyone*

The tools change monthly. We run regular live sessions for staff across the business: effective
prompting, verifying output, building personal workflows, and knowing when not to trust the
result. You get a workforce that keeps getting better with AI instead of falling behind.

### Automation & Agentic Systems
*Identify, scope, build*

We work with your team to find the workflows worth automating, measure the bottleneck, scope the
solution, and build it to production standards — with evaluations, guardrails, and vendor-portable
architecture. If the honest answer is "not yet," we'll say so and show you what has to be ready
first. You get systems that deliver measurable
value and survive model changes.
