## 2. Day Schedule & Content

**Overall Arc:** "What AI can do for you" (morning) → "How AI fits into serious
engineering" (afternoon)

### Morning Session — "AI for Your Business" (9:00 AM – 12:30 PM)

#### 9:00 – 9:15 | Welcome & Framing

- Who BSF5Y is (brief, not a pitch)
- What this day is about: honest look at AI tools — what works, what doesn't, where the line is
- Set expectations: demos are real, not cherry-picked; you'll see failures too

#### 9:15 – 10:15 | The AI Tool Landscape

Broad survey with named tools. Live demos across categories, ~10–12 minutes each:

- **Writing & Editorial:** Grammar, tone adjustment, reading-level targeting, drafting business copy (e.g., Claude, ChatGPT)
- **Imagery & Branding:** Logo concepts, social media graphics, color palette generation (e.g., Midjourney, DALL-E)
- **Document Creation:** Slide decks, proposals, reports, white papers (e.g., Claude with artifacts, Gamma, Google Gemini)
- **Website Construction:** HTML/CSS/JS generation, accessibility, rapid prototyping (e.g., Claude Code, Bolt, v0)
- **Business Intelligence:** Insight mining from existing data, structuring data capture for the future (e.g., Claude with CSV/spreadsheet analysis)

#### 10:15 – 10:30 | Break

#### 10:30 – 11:15 | Creativity, Integrity & Limitations

- What does "AI-generated" mean for your brand authenticity?
- Copyright, attribution, and IP considerations
- Where AI confidently gets it wrong (hallucinations, bias, fabricated sources)
- Keeping AI in its lane: what to trust, what to verify, what to never delegate
- Practical guardrails for your team

#### 11:15 – 11:45 | What Does It Cost?

- Subscription tiers and pricing across major platforms
- Hidden costs: time to learn, time to review output, integration effort
- ROI framing: where AI saves real hours vs. where it creates busywork
- The "build vs. buy vs. AI" decision framework

#### 11:45 – 12:00 | Why This Matters for Your Business

- AI as this generation's differentiating efficiency technology
- The competitive landscape: businesses that adopt vs. those that don't
- Starting small: low-risk, high-value entry points for any SMB

#### 12:00 – 12:30 | Lunch + Community Signup + Survey Intro

- Catered lunch / break
- Sign up for BSF5Y community list (mailing list, future events)
- Introduce the post-event survey: "Tell us what topics you'd come back for"
- Natural exit point for those not staying for the afternoon

### Afternoon Session — "AI for Your Engineering" (1:00 PM – 4:00 PM)

#### 1:00 – 1:15 | Setting the Stage

- Quick round of intros — who's still in the room, what they're hoping to learn
- Frame the afternoon: AI doesn't replace engineering discipline — it amplifies it. You have to get your practices right first.

#### 1:15 – 2:00 | Software Engineering with AI — The Prerequisites

- **Process/Workflow Requirements:** Version control, code review, CI/CD — AI accelerates good practices, it multiplies bad ones
- **Cloud/Infrastructure Requirements:** Where AI tooling lives, what it needs, how it integrates
- **Security, Reliability, and Velocity:** AI-generated code still needs testing, auditing, and review. "Go well to go fast."
- For non-engineers: BSF5Y's turnkey infrastructure solutions — this is what you hire someone to handle

#### 2:00 – 2:45 | Live Demo: AI-Assisted Development Workflow

- Walk through a realistic development task using AI tools end-to-end
- Show the full loop: prompt → generate → review → test → refine
- Highlight where human judgment is irreplaceable
- Tools shown: Claude Code, GitHub Copilot, or similar

#### 2:45 – 3:00 | Break

#### 3:00 – 3:30 | Evangelism & Change Management

- How to introduce AI tooling to a resistant team
- Managing expectations: what AI adoption actually looks like in the first 90 days
- Common pitfalls: over-reliance, under-training, skipping the "learning to prompt" phase

#### 3:30 – 3:50 | Future Topics & Open Discussion

- What topics would you want a deeper workshop on? (Capture list for future events)
- Open Q&A — let the room drive
- This directly feeds future event planning

#### 3:50 – 4:00 | Wrap-Up & Next Steps

- Post-event survey (hand out or QR code)
- BSF5Y consultation offer: "If any of this sparked an idea, let's talk" (low-pressure, teacher tone)
- Community signup reminder
- Thank the sponsor

**Total runtime:** ~6.5 hours (with breaks and lunch)

## 3. Outcomes & Success Metrics

### Attendee Outcomes

- Understand the current AI tool landscape and what's actually useful vs. hype
- Know the practical costs (money, time, risk) of adopting AI tools
- Have a mental framework for where AI fits in their business and where it doesn't
- (Afternoon) Understand what engineering discipline AI requires and what BSF5Y offers as turnkey solutions

### BSF5Y Outcomes

- Establish thought leadership in the local tech/business community
- Build a warm contact list (community signups) for future engagement
- Identify potential consulting/training clients through post-event conversations and consultation requests
- Gather structured data on local demand for AI-related services and education (survey)
- Collect a ranked list of follow-up/deep-dive topics to inform future events

### Success Metrics

- 15+ attendees registered, 12+ show up
- 50%+ stay for the afternoon session
- 80%+ complete the post-event survey
- 5+ actionable topic suggestions for future events
- 2–3 consultation requests within 2 weeks of the event
- Qualitative: did it feel like a community event, not a sales pitch?

### Post-Event Follow-Up

- Send thank-you email within 24 hours with survey link (for those who didn't complete on-site)
- Share a summary of the day (key takeaways, tools mentioned, links) — gives people a reason to stay subscribed
- Publish survey results (anonymized) as a blog post or community update — demonstrates transparency and builds content
- Follow up individually with anyone who requested a consultation
