# Bootstrap Your In-House AI Tooling

## What

One day event split into two 'programs'.  The morning/pre-lunch session/s will cater to the business
advantages of leveraging AI in the Small to Medium sized business space.

### Morning Session

- What is AI, and what tools exist.
  - Creative writing, grammar, editorial advices, tone adjustment, reading-'age' adjustment
  - Imagry, messaging, branding, color pallettes, ...
  - Website Construction (HTML, CSS, Javascript, etc), Accessibility, ...
  - Document creation: Slide Decks, Proposals, Reports, Scientific/White papers, ...
  - Business Intelligence: Insight mining of existing IT data, future proof data catpture
    practices...

- Why AI will be critical to your business's future success
  - Efficiency has always been key to competition, AI is our generation's differentiating tech
  - Businesses will be consumed by competitors or displaced by rapidly moving startups

- What does it cost?

### Afternoon Session

- Creativity and Integrity while using AI

- Keeping AI in its lane

- Software Engineering w/ AI
  - Must get your practices up to snuff first
  - Process/Workflow Requirements 
  - Cloud/Infrastruture Requirements
  - Security, Reliability, and Max Velocity

- Evangelism and Change Management

## Who

- Decision makers in Regional Start-ups and Small to Medium Sized Businesses
- Entrepreneurs interested in Self-building their MVPs
- Established engineers intersted in leveling-up thier understanding/skill sets
