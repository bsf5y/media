# The bsf5y Engagement Model: A Partnership for Success

This document outlines our collaborative approach to building exceptional products. It serves as both a guide for our clients, setting clear expectations for our journey together, and as a playbook for our
internal teams to ensure we deliver with consistency and excellence. Our process is designed to move beyond ideas and prototypes to deliver robust, market-ready products with a clear strategic advantage.

## Phase 0: Discovery and Clarity
*"Show us the vision."*

Share the product idea with us like we could be its biggest customer.

- What does v. 1.0 (or perhaps 2.0) of the product do?  What does it look/feel like to its users?
- What will give the product credibility in the market?
- What strengths does the existing product team bring to the product?  What gaps exist in the team?
- What is the mission objective?  Why is this product being developed?

## Phase 1: Differentiation
*"What can only this product do?"*

Once the scope is clear, we identify what's actually unique — the defensible and valuable core:

- Is it proprietary data? A novel algorithm? Domain expertise encoded in software?
- What's the core value that competitors can't easily replicate?

## Phase 2: Foundation / Infrastructure Requirements
*"Turnkey the rest."*

Identify what is required to deliver the product across all user channels with the appropriate
level of reliability and redundancy to meet all quality assurance needs.

Everything that isn't part of the core value is infrastructure. Web UI, apps, authentication, payments, admin
dashboards, CI/CD, cloud infrastructure, etc — these all have mature and proven turnkey solutions.  We
provide prior art and battle-tested solutions to all infrastructure requirements at a fraction of
the cost of building them from scratch.

Foundations extend to the development infrastructure.  This includes the AI-driven development
systems necessary to maintain market advantage as product development cycles continue to accelerate.

## Phase 3: Execution
*"Products, not prototypes."*

With clarity, differentiation, and foundation in place, we build a robust core solution for the
product: exhaustive test coverage, clean software architecture, comprehensive documentation,
engineering artifacts, and processes that we can hand to future teams without apology.

## Phase 4: Future-Proofing
*"Building for tomorrow, not just for today."*

The goal for the future may be to:

- cross-train an existing engineering team
- hire completely new development staff
- create product management teams skilled in managing AI-Driven Development systems
- engage bsf5y to build, retain, manage, and support a dedicated team

These paths aren't mutually exclusive. We work with clients to craft the right combination —
ensuring that the product remains secure, resilient, flexible, and adaptable to whatever change
market forces demand.
